About Me

I Capture Souls,
Not Just Faces

I'm Elara Voss — a visual artist and retoucher based between Paris and New York. My work lives at the intersection of technical precision and raw emotion, translating fleeting moments into timeless imagery.

With over a decade of experience in editorial, commercial, and beauty photography, I've collaborated with leading magazines, agencies, and luxury brands. Every frame is a deliberate choice; every retouch, a quiet conversation between the image and its truth.
